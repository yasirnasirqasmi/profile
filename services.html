07Common
questions
Do you work with clients outside Saudi Arabia?

Yes. I am based in Jeddah and work in Saudi hours, but a good share of my clients are in Pakistan, the UAE and further afield. Everything runs over WhatsApp, email and a shared staging link, so location rarely matters.

Who owns the code when the project ends?

You do. On handover you receive the full source, the database, and every credential — hosting, domain, email, third-party keys. Nothing is locked to me, and you are free to hire anyone else to continue the work.

Can you take over a system another developer built?

Often, yes. I start with a paid audit so both of us know what we are dealing with before committing — sometimes the honest answer is that a rebuild costs less than the repair, and I would rather tell you that upfront than discover it halfway through.

How long does a typical project take?

Most builds run two to six weeks from approved scope to live system. A marketplace launch is usually two to four weeks depending on how fast verification clears. SEO is different — technical fixes show inside a month, but ranking movement realistically takes three to six.

Can you work in Arabic?

I build bilingual Arabic and English systems, including right-to-left layouts and bilingual printed documents, and I write Arabic marketplace and SEO content. My spoken Arabic is basic, so project conversations are in English or Urdu.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings or sales figures?

No, and be cautious of anyone who does. Rankings and marketplace sales depend on competition, budget and product factors outside any contractor's control. What I commit to is the work itself — the fixes, the listings, the reporting — and showing you exactly what moved each month.
